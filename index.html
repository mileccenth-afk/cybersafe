CyberSafe is an educational learning platform designed to equip students with critical skills against online scams, phishing attempts, data leaks, and cyber vulnerabilities. Learn, test your knowledge, and practice responsible technology use.

Why Student Cybersecurity Matters

Students are primary targets for cybercriminals due to constant online presence and high usage of social media and campus Wi-Fi networks.

Phishing & Impersonation

Fake scholarship emails, bogus account verification notices, and social engineering scams targeting student credentials.

Weak Credentials

Reusing passwords across academic portals, entertainment sites, and banking apps significantly escalates identity theft risk.

Unsecured Networks

Accessing sensitive personal accounts over public library or campus Wi-Fi without encryption exposes data to interception.

Professional Ethics & Social Responsibility

Understanding legal responsibilities, data privacy laws, and ethical tech behavior.

Data Privacy & Information Protection

IT professionals and students handling personal data must abide by privacy fundamentals (e.g., Data Privacy Act standards). This includes ensuring consent, confidentiality, and data minimization when building applications or managing student records.

Responsible Disclosure & Ethics

Discovering a security vulnerability in a campus website or online tool requires Responsible Disclosure. Exploiting security flaws for personal gain or unauthorized access is illegal and violates academic ethical standards.

The Student Digital Code of Conduct

1. Respect Privacy
Never share personal credentials, ID numbers, or photos of classmates online without explicit consent.
2. Practice Integrity
Avoid downloading or spreading pirated software, malware tools, or keyloggers on shared hardware.
3. Report Threats
Notify campus IT departments when encountering suspicious emails, phishing links, or compromised portals.
